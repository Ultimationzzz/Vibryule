# App icon

`vibryule.png` is the final AI-generated artwork, created with ChatGPT's built-in image generation tool. `vibryule.ico` contains 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixel frames for Windows. The ICO is a size/format conversion of the PNG with its transparency preserved.

Direction and attribution: Ultimation and ChatGPT. Zelda-inspired fan artwork for this unofficial viewer; no artwork was extracted from the game.

## Final generation prompt

Edit this draft Windows app icon for Vibryule, a Zelda Ocarina of Time 3D scene viewer, to make it unmistakably Zelda-inspired. Replace the oversized plain V and blue technology badge with a bold forest-green Hylian-style shield with a thick metallic gold rim, a large clearly readable gold Triforce in the upper center, and a simple stylized gold V incorporated in the lower shield. A small, broad emerald isometric terrain shape at the shield base can hint at a world viewer. Use rich forest green, warm gold and restrained deep navy shadows, polished graphic game-icon styling with subtle dimensional shading. Keep the design very simple, a strong recognizable silhouette readable at 16 and 32 pixels. Single centered icon, square canvas, shield fills most of the canvas with safe margins. Real transparent background outside the shield. No words, no mockup, no tiny ornaments, no watermark.
